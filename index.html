About

How I read

Tarot, in my hands, is a mirror. The cards show what is present in a situation and what is unfolding from it. Your choices decide how the path develops from there. I read them with honesty, and I deliver what I see plainly and with care, the way a trusted friend would if she happened to keep a deck on the table.

A reading with me ends with something you can do. One conversation to have, one boundary to hold, one truth to sit with. You keep the pen on your own story. My work is to help you see the page clearly.

What I Hold To

The ethics under every reading

  • Your free will comes first. The cards inform a choice. You make it.
  • Honesty, delivered kindly. You will hear what I see, including the parts that are hard to say, held with warmth.
  • You leave stronger than you came. A good reading returns your power to you. My aim is that you need me less over time, and want the clarity more.
  • Spiritual insight, in its lane. A reading supports your decisions. For medical, legal, financial, or mental-health matters, the right licensed professional is the person to see.
